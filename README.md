# AaryanSharma_CMSC131

Line 1 of Story (written by Angel Moreno): A cloud with an angry face was starting to bring rain and thunder over a city.
Line 2 of Story (written by Kwabena Appiah-Dankwah): The people of the city were drenched and the city was starting to flood. They had no idea what to do.
Line 3 of Story (written by Aaryan Sharma): Luckily, Rain Man showed up to stop the rain! Using his rain-reversing powers, he cleared the flood and saved the city!
